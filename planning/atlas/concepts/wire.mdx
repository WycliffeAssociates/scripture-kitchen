## Why

- **No mirrors.** Before 0.1.0, `onion-wasm.ts` held 109 hand-mirrored constants (commit b781bb9). Generating both ends from one declaration took that to zero.
- **A consumer never learns a layout.** Each reader checks magic, version and strides at `open` and throws naming the mismatch, so a reader a version behind fails loudly instead of misreading a field (`architecture.md`).
- **Little-endian by construction.** A field declares its name, width, whether it is a source offset (`Space::Offset`, converted under `utf16`), and the expression that reaches it. Nothing casts a Rust struct.
- **Checked in at both ends.** `codegen_output_matches_input` fails the build when either end is stale.

Debt, named in `architecture.md`: the [[corpus]] envelope and sous-core's reader are still partly typed by hand.
