## Why

Sefer is the consumer most kitchen work is shaped by. Its numbered asks appear in commits and planning items: resolve (asks 12, 14), `OnionBook::locate` cost (11), `LetterRun` (13), stable diagnostic identity (15). Its `locate.ts` was the reference [[resolve]] ported, with the departures listed in `resolve.md`.

It vendors the [[galley-package]], the superset module that carries every onion door beside the `Galley` handle (`galley/README.md`), so a new door must land on galley. Offsets at the wall are UTF-16 code units, the editor's unit.

Sefer's own repo is outside this workspace; its internals are not documented here.
