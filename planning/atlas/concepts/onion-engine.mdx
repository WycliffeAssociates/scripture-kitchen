## Why

- **Source is the truth.** Every product is an index over or a derivation from the exact bytes ([[source]]); round trip is identity by construction.
- **One engine, many drivers.** Native Rust callers use [[onion]]; JS uses [[onion-wasm]] or the [[galley-package]]. The engine never forks per host (GLOSSARY, "Engine").
- **Stateless.** Text in, numbers and strings out. A stateful handle was ruled out until a profiler shows the per-call index build matters (`onion-wasm/README.md`, "Deliberately absent").
- **Owns scripture structure for Sous** too: parsing, marker validity, canonical order, masks, verse anchors (`charter.md`).

## Composes with

Built from the [[onion]] crate; the [[cst]] is its base. [[sous-chef]] reads it only through [[galley]]'s [[onion-book]].
