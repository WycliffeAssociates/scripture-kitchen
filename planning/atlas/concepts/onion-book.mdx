## The problem

sous-chef looks for inconsistencies by counting words and verses. It needs each book as plain verse text, with chapter and verse positions, and a way to point a finding back at the real file. But sous-chef is meant to work without the USFM parser, so it cannot reach into the parser's types. Something has to translate between them.

## The solution

sous-chef defines a small, neutral shape it accepts: a book key, its text, its chapters and its verses (`ProjectedBook`). This adapter builds that shape from the parser's verse-text [[mask]] and its index of chapters and verses (the [[toc]]). It lives in [[galley]], the one crate allowed to depend on both sides. A finding maps back to the file as a list of pieces, not one span, because a finding can cross a removed footnote.

## Trade-offs

- Verses must ascend. A verse out of order is left out of the analysis (it abstains); [[lint]] reports it instead.
- The adapter keeps the longest ascending run, so one stray verse number drops only that verse, not its neighbours.
- sous-chef sees only plain text and numbers. It cannot reason about markup.

## Show me

From `galley/src/sous/onion_book.rs`. The footnote is gone from the text, and `locate` returns two raw pieces around it:

```text
OnionBook::parse("\\id MRK\n\\c 1\n\\p\n\\v 1 Jesus \\f + \\ft note\\f* wept.\n")
  .text()      "Jesus  wept.\n"          // verse text, markup gone
  .chapters()  [Chapter 1 at 0..13]
  .locate(0..13)                          // -> MRK 1:1, raw runs [21..27, 43..50]
```

Out-of-order verses abstain:

```text
\v 22 \v 23 \v 24 \v 23 \v 25   →  the second 23 abstains
\v 22 \v 23 \v 42 \v 25 \v 26   →  42 abstains, not 25 and 26
```

## How it works

- **sous-core never depends on onion.** It takes a borrowed `ProjectedBook`. Onion stays the USFM authority; Sous sees projected ranges and numeric units (`charter.md`, "Ownership boundaries").
- **Ascending verses** come from a patience sort keeping the longest ascending run.
- **`from_parts` rebuilds from retained products** without lexing again; `locate` walks only the ranges it returns (Sefer ask 11, planning item 2026-10-20).

## Composes with

Built from a [[mask]] and [[toc]]; consumed by [[chapter-pass]]es in [[sous-core]]; built per book by the [[expediter]] and the cold `publish_onion_findings`, whose results become the [[findings-snapshot]].
