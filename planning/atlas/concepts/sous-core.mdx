## Why

- **Never depends on onion.** It accepts a small neutral borrowed view, not a parse. "Do not add the dependency" (`architecture.md`). A vref corpus can produce the same view.
- **Owns content evidence only.** Unicode classification, config-free observations, corpus reductions, judging, finding serialization. Not editor positions, persistence, I/O or localization (`charter.md`, "Sous owns content evidence").
- **No resident caches.** Galley owns reuse; sous-core declares what is safe to reuse (`SCHEMA`, `RETAIN_CHAPTERS`).
- **Its own reader emitter.** sous-core generates `sous-reader.ts` itself rather than through [[ticket]]; its numbers are generated and its reader typed (`architecture.md` names this as debt).

## Composes with

Depends on [[mise]]. Depended on by [[galley]] and [[sous-cli]]. Defines the [[chapter-pass]] contract and the [[findings-snapshot]]; reads [[onion-book]]s through `ProjectedBook`; splits words by [[words]].
