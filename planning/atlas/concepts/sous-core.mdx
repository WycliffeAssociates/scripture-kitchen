## The problem

Sous's counts and verdicts must work for any producer of addressed verse text (onion's mask today, a vref file too), and must be reusable across keystrokes without owning a cache.

## The solution

It accepts a borrowed `ProjectedBook`, text plus chapter and verse rows, and never imports onion ([[invariants]] 7); [[galley]] builds the view. Rules are [[chapter-pass]]es whose map is config-free and pure per chapter, so galley caches by content while sous-core only declares what is safe to reuse ([[invariants]] 4, 5). Judging is explicit fractions against support bands, not a score. Its only shared dependency is [[mise]].

## Trade-offs

- It cannot ask onion anything; any structural fact it needs must be in the neutral view.
- It owns content evidence only. Editor positions, persistence, I/O and localisation are elsewhere.
- Its snapshot reader is typed by hand with only the numbers generated, not built on [[ticket]]: listed as debt.

## How it works

- **Never depends on onion.** "Do not add the dependency" (`architecture.md`). A vref corpus can produce the same view.
- **Owns content evidence only.** Unicode classification, config-free observations, corpus reductions, judging, finding serialization (`charter.md`, "Sous owns content evidence").
- **No resident caches.** sous-core declares what is safe to reuse (`SCHEMA`, `RETAIN_CHAPTERS`).
- **Its own reader emitter.** Its numbers are generated and its reader typed.
