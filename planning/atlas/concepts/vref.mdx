## Show me

`playground --vref` on the scratch book in [[cst]]:

```text
MRK 1:1	Jesus wept. Then
MRK 1:2	he Lord   spoke.
MRK 1:3	<range>
MRK 1:4	Bread.
```

## Why

- **Line N names the same verse in every translation.** That is ebible's shape and the whole point. A bridge puts its text on its first verse and gives each covered verse a `<range>` line; omitting them would slide every later verse onto the wrong line.
- **No second filter.** Which bytes are verse text was decided by the [[mask]] handed in. The renderer only does what the format demands: a newline or tab becomes one space, byte for byte.
- **Renders what the file holds.** Blank lines for verses a book never had need a versification table onion does not own (see planning item 2026-10-14, versification layer).

## Composes with

Reads the [[toc]] and a [[mask]]; bridges come from the [[designator]]'s hull. Used by `playground --vref` for `debug/` dumps.
