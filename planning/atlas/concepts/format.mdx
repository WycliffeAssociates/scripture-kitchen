## Show me

`playground --format-trace`, default options, on the scratch book in [[cst]]: 3 edits over 125 bytes.

```text
before                                   after
\p                                       \p \v 1 Jesus wept.\f + \ft why\f* Then \v 2-3 he \nd Lord\nd* spoke.
\v 1 Jesus wept.\f + \ft why\f* Then     \s1 A heading
\v 2-3 he \nd Lord\nd*   spoke.          \q1 \v 4 Bread.
\s1 A heading
\q1 \v 4 Bread.
```

`verse_breaks: Remove` joins verses onto their paragraph's line; `VerseBreaks::Keep` would keep them.

## Why

- **Edits, not a rewriter.** Format runs [[lint]], keeps the fixes of rows flagged `formatter` plus any named `repairs`, then a second walk evaluates the Form-channel rows. The two sets merge and collide by row order: first writer wins, the loser is not emitted.
- **The one pass allowed to invent bytes.** It invents whitespace and engine fix strings, never prose. The [[mask]]'s no-normalization law does not bind it.
- **Scope engine-side, never in JS.** `format_edits_in` runs the same whole-book analysis and filters to a window. An edit straddling the edge is dropped whole; a multi-edit claim (`bridge-empty-verses`) is kept only if all of it is inside (`onion-wasm/README.md`).

Trade-off: scoping to a chapter still pays for the whole book's lint.

## Composes with

Reads [[lint]] and the Form channel over the same rows. Emits [[edit]]s. Crosses as `onion-wasm`'s `Edits` handle with UTF-16 spans ([[onion-wasm]]).
