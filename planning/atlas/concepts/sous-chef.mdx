## The problem

Teams proofread languages with no dictionary, model or style guide, and conventions vary: French spaces inside guillemets, Amharic writes `::` for its full stop, many scripts have no case. A rule list (ripgrep for spaced closers or doubled punctuation) fires on every such project's correct text.

## The solution

Learn each convention from the project and flag departures ([[invariants]] 7). Glyph neighbours, placement, run shapes and rarity, and word casing, doubling and length, are counted; a verdict is a fraction of the project's own population ("1 of 1,053") that must clear a support floor (default 5) and a share band scaled to the denominator. A `` ,` `` pair against 5,000 plain commas fires on run shape (1 in 5,001, band 100 bp); French guillemet spacing, Amharic `::` and uncased scripts abstain with no allow-list. Band defaults come from a sweep of 1,504 fleet corpora. Hygiene (control characters, U+FFFD, conflict markers) is the deterministic exception, and length proportionality compares against a source.

## Trade-offs

- It reports rare, not wrong; a rare but correct form needs a person to dismiss it.
- Thin evidence abstains, and abstaining is not a claim that the text is fine.
- Reasons stay separate. There is no blended confidence score.

## How it works

The product promise (`charter.md`):

- **Narrow, truthful claims.** "This form occurs 1 of 1,053 times" is valid; "this is wrong" usually is not.
- **Every verdict shows its numerator, denominator, population and abstention condition.** Unrelated signals are not blended into a confidence number.
- **Patterns first, sites second.** One review row may expand to every matching span.
- **Useful on a new language from the first corpus.** No language model, dictionary or trained artifact ships.
- **Structure comes from Onion.** Sous does not parse USFM; it reads a neutral projection [[galley]] builds.

Measurements behind each rule are in `sous-chef/evidence.md`; checks are defined in `sous-chef/rules/`.
