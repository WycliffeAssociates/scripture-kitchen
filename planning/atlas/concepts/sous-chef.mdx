## Why

The product promise (`charter.md`):

- **Narrow, truthful claims.** "This form occurs 1 of 1,053 times" is valid; "this is wrong" usually is not.
- **Every verdict shows its numerator, denominator, population and abstention condition.** Unrelated signals are not blended into a confidence number.
- **Patterns first, sites second.** One review row may expand to every matching span.
- **Useful on a new language from the first corpus.** No language model, dictionary or trained artifact ships.

Measurements behind each rule are in `sous-chef/evidence.md`; checks are defined in `sous-chef/rules/`.

## Composes with

The [[sous-core]] crate does the counting; [[sous-cli]] and the [[galley-package]] host it. Structure comes from the [[onion-engine]] through [[galley]]; findings leave as the [[findings-snapshot]] built by the [[expediter]].
