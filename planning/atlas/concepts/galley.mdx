```text
galley::pantry      the one caching layer (chunks, books, derived stores, budget)

galley::corpus      the ONCP envelope

```

## Why

- **The only place the engines meet.** "Anything that might be considered stateful lives here and NEVER in the engines" (`galley/src/lib.rs`). `galley::sous` owns the seam entire.
- **One caching layer.** Everything kept between calls is in the [[pantry]] or a store it defines (`architecture.md`).
- **The JS superset.** galley's cdylib links onion-wasm's shims, so one module exports `Galley` and every onion door. A test pins the exact export list because a cdylib silently drops what nothing references.
- **Depends on** [[onion]], [[sous-core]], [[mise]], [[ticket]]; [[onion-wasm]] and `wasm-bindgen` only under `wasm`; `rayon` only under `parallel`.

Doc drift: `galley/src/lib.rs`'s diagram and `onion-wasm/README.md` still name a `sous-wasm` crate and a "five-crate layout"; the workspace has seven members and no `sous-wasm`.

## Composes with

Implements the [[pantry]], [[chunk-cache]], [[expediter]], [[onion-book]], [[find]], [[overlay]] and the [[census]], [[mask-map]], [[find-buffer]] and [[corpus]] wires. Ships as the [[galley-package]].
