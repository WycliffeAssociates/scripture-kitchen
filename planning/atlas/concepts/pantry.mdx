## Why

- **`update` is the only door that takes a book's text.** Replacement is whole-book under a caller-chosen id; there is no splice API, so coordinates cannot shift inside a book. Marshaling a 5 MB corpus into wasm costs 3.5–5 ms, a quarter of a frame, while retaining 5 MB is cheap (`analysis-host.md`).
- **Roles are asymmetric, and measured.** On en_ulb a Target's products are 7.29 MB (162% of 4.51 MB raw, text included); a Reference's are 1.17 MB for lengths alone, 3.94 MB with the word lane.
- **A Target cannot drop its text.** Placing a finding rescans the text, so `Retain::ProductsOnly` on a Target is refused (`PantryError::TargetNeedsText`).
- **Three tiers, no residual.** Pinned (text, `Toc`, `Mask`, `Utf16Table`, `Fingerprint`), hot (the [[expediter]]'s hot-set rows) and rebuildable (chunks and derived stores). Only the [[chunk-cache]] is evicted today; a counting allocator holds the total to a tenth of live bytes over a whole Bible.
- **Dirty is not rework.** A moved chapter makes the file dirty but needs no re-derivation. One `Fingerprint`, ~1 KB of chunk starts and xxh3-128s, answers both.
- **Canonical order** by `BookKey` from [[mise]], ties by id, so `BookIndex` never depends on update order.

Doc conflict: `analysis-host.md` says "the `Reference` role [is] not built"; `pantry.md` and the code (`Role::Reference`) have it live.
