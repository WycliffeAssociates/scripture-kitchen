## The problem

Onion and Sous need the same book codes and order, offset types and word rule, and Sous may not depend on onion.

## The solution

Put them in a leaf with no dependencies that both import ([[invariants]] 7); copies would drift. The scope rule: only what more than one crate needs, and only spec tables, borrow-free structures and versioned text rules. Generators stay out.

## Trade-offs

- No dependencies at all, so nothing needing a hash map or I/O. The Unicode table lives here; its generator stays in [[sous-core]].
- Holding the bytes is not owning the meaning: onion still owns canonical order as a rule.

## How it works

- **The boundary needs a leaf.** onion and sous-core must share `BookKey` and the UTF-16 offset types without either depending on the other (`charter.md`).
- **Scope rule** (`mise/README.md`): standard-derived tables, borrow-free structures, versioned text rules. No I/O, no policy, no checksums, no engine logic, no dependencies.
- **A generated table may live here while its generator does not.** `bin/gen-unicode` needs a hash map and the UCD extracts, so it stays in sous-core and writes across.

## Composes with

Depended on by [[onion]], [[onion-wasm]], [[galley]] and [[sous-core]]. Holds [[utf16]], [[words]], the [[extensions]] list format and [[resolve]]'s `Address`.
