## Why

- **The boundary needs a leaf.** onion and sous-core must share `BookKey` and the UTF-16 offset types without either depending on the other (`charter.md`). mise depends on nothing.
- **Scope rule** (`mise/README.md`): only things more than one crate needs, and only of three kinds: standard-derived tables, borrow-free structures, versioned text rules. No I/O, no policy, no checksums, no engine logic, no dependencies.
- **Owning bytes is not owning semantics.** Onion still owns canonical order as a rule; Sous still owns what a book key means to a corpus.
- **A generated table may live here while its generator does not.** `bin/gen-unicode` needs a hash map and the UCD extracts, so it stays in sous-core and writes across.
