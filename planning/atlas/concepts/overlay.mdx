## The problem

Many translations are drafted as bare verses: verse markers and text, with no paragraph or poetry breaks. A translator wants them laid out like a source Bible, so Genesis 2:23 reads as four lines of poetry. Doing it by hand is slow, and guessing where to break a sentence in another language is impossible to do reliably.

## The solution

We read the source's layout as a list of breaks, each addressed by verse: "a paragraph starts before verse 24", "the second poetry line inside verse 23". Verse numbers mean the same thing in every language, so breaks before a verse land exactly. Breaks inside a verse cannot be placed by word, so we insert them empty after the verse's text, and the translator pastes the words in. We do not guess by text length or proportion. The result is one set of [[edit]]s, offered as a suggestion, never as a problem in the book.

## Trade-offs

- Breaks inside a verse arrive empty; the translator still has to move text into them.
- Footnotes are not copied across.
- The source text must be kept loaded in the [[pantry]] (`keepText`).
- It is not part of publishing; it only runs when asked.

## Show me

From `galley/src/overlay.md` (GEN 2:21–24). The source's six breaks become six inserts in the target:

```text
skeleton(source)
  GEN 2:21 leading 1 p       GEN 2:23 inside 1 q1     GEN 2:23 inside 2 q2
  GEN 2:23 inside 3 q1       GEN 2:23 inside 4 q2     GEN 2:24 leading 1 p

overlay(target, source) → 6 edits, ascending, non-overlapping
  insert "\p\n"   at the target's \v 21
  insert "\n\q1" "\n\q2" "\n\q1" "\n\q2"   after verse 23's text
  insert "\p\n"   at the target's \v 24
```

## How it works

- **Address = (verse sid, leading | inside, ordinal)**, plus the marker as a check.
- **Empty blocks fold by onion's own [[lint]]** (`Code::EmptyParagraph`), not a second copy of the rule.
- **Invariant:** apply the edits, re-extract, and the target's skeleton equals the source's with empty blocks folded. `tests/overlay.rs` states the comparison itself so it cannot drift into agreement with the library.
- Verses are paired through `sous_core::align` over both sides' [[onion-book]]s.

## Composes with

Reads the [[cst]], [[lint]] report and [[mask]] from the [[pantry]]. Pairs verses through `sous_core::align` over both sides' [[onion-book]]s. Emits [[edit]]s as onion-wasm's `Edits` class.
