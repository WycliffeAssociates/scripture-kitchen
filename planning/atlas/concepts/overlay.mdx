## Show me

From `galley/src/overlay.md` (GEN 2:21–24):

```text
skeleton(source)
  GEN 2:21 leading 1 p       GEN 2:23 inside 1 q1     GEN 2:23 inside 2 q2
  GEN 2:23 inside 3 q1       GEN 2:23 inside 4 q2     GEN 2:24 leading 1 p

overlay(target, source) → 6 edits, ascending, non-overlapping
  insert "\p\n"   at the target's \v 21
  insert "\n\q1" "\n\q2" "\n\q1" "\n\q2"   after verse 23's text
  insert "\p\n"   at the target's \v 24
```

## Why

- **Address = (verse sid, leading | inside, ordinal)**, plus the marker as a check. "Immediately before `\v 24`" means the same in every language, so leading blocks land exactly. Where a verse's text splits is unknowable across languages, so inside blocks are inserted empty after the verse and the translator pastes into them.
- **Nothing by proportion or length ratio.**
- **Notes never cross.** They are not blocks.
- **Empty blocks fold by onion's own lint** (`Code::EmptyParagraph`), not a second copy of the rule.
- **Invariant:** apply the edits, re-extract, and the target's skeleton equals the source's with empty blocks folded. `tests/overlay.rs` states the comparison itself so it cannot drift into agreement with the library.
- **A suggestion, never a finding.** Not on the publication path.

## Composes with

Reads the [[cst]], [[lint]] report and [[mask]] from the [[pantry]] (the source needs `keepText`). Pairs verses through `sous_core::align` over both sides' [[onion-book]]s. Emits [[edit]]s as onion-wasm's `Edits` class.
