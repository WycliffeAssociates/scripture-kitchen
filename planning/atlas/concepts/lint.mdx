## Why

Three laws from `lint.rs`:

- **Read verdicts, never re-derive them.** `unclosed-note` is the [[walker]]'s `Recovery` on the `\f` node, matched, not recomputed.
- **Flag, never repair.** No token is moved, inserted or dropped. A `Fix` is an offered run of [[edit]]s. Moves and guesses are not offered (relocating an attribute list, guessing a book code).
- **No strings.** An `Observation` is four `u32`s: anchor and second are token indices, `aux` means what the row's `aux` column says. A report crosses as one flat array; text comes from `diagnostics.json`.

`diagnostics.json` lists 59 codes in six categories (structure, ordering, attributes, payload, form, version). 11 have severity `form`: the Form channel, which only [[format]] evaluates. The `lint.rs` doc's "48 codes" is the count without them. ~9.4 ns/token on en_ulb, ~15.5 on en_ult; the gap is the k/v attribute rules over 31 MB of `\w` interiors.

Severity can depend on the declared `\usfm` version, so `declared_version` rides on every report and two rules are gated on it.
