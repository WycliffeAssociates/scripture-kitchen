## The problem

Reviewing a colleague's copy of a book needs answers per verse ("verse 3 changed"). A line diff cannot give that: one reflowed paragraph is dozens of changed lines.

## The solution

Cut both sides at the [[toc]]'s chapter and verse anchors (no re-scan), align by verse identity, and report each unit as modified, added or deleted; word or character detail is opt-in inside a modified unit. Merge is "take this side" per unit, written out as text or as [[edit]]s. Moves are not detected: a moved verse is a delete plus an add.

## Trade-offs

- JSON, not a binary wire ([[invariants]] 2 does not hold here): word detail grows 66-book JSON from 221 KB to 6.2 MB, 17 ms of `JSON.parse`.
- A moved verse reads as a delete plus an add.
- Word boundaries never cross a footnote edge.

## How it works

- **Three layers, one walk.** Identity: a block is the bytes between two [[toc]] anchors, a `Range<u32>` plus a `Copy` address. Alignment: Myers over 12-byte addresses, then coalescing pairs a rebridged or relabeled verse into one decision. Projection: `merge` emits bytes, [[code:onion/src/diff.rs#to_edits]] emits replay splices.
- **Changed units only** unless `unchanged: true`.
- **Runs are asked for by name.** `"none"`, `"words"` ([[words]]: each word, line break, whitespace run and other scalar is one unit) or `"chars"`; an unknown name rejects. The [[cst]] and the `text` [[mask]] are built only when runs are asked for.
- **JSON at the wall.** serde keeps the old editors' camelCase contract; there is no binary run wire.

## Composes with

Reads the [[toc]] per side, and the [[cst]] and the `text` [[mask]] cut only when runs are asked for. Emits [[edit]]s (`to_edits`, `SpliceEdit`) for replay. Contrast with [[plate]]: diff is the exception to "nothing rich crosses".
