# scripture-kitchen

One Rust workspace, two products, one JS package.

- **[[onion-engine|Onion]]** reads USFM losslessly: [[scanner]] to [[token]]s, tokens to the [[cst]], and everything else derived from those.
- **[[sous-chef|Sous Chef]]** finds reviewable inconsistencies from counts alone, and never depends on onion.
- **[[galley]]** is the one crate allowed to see both. It caches ([[pantry]]), publishes ([[expediter]]) and ships the [[galley-package]] that [[sefer]] runs.

## Four zoom levels

| level | what | start with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | products and consumers | [[onion-engine]], [[sous-chef]], [[galley-package]], [[sefer]] |
| 1 | crates, and who may depend on whom | [[mise]], [[ticket]], [[onion]], [[galley]], [[sous-core]] |

## Where to start

Read the [[cst]]. It is the base nearly everything reads: [[mask]], [[lint]], the [[exports]] and the [[dish]]. Then [[toc]] (where things are, no tree needed), then [[chunk]] and the [[chunk-cache]] (how a keystroke avoids re-reading a book).

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  source --> scanner --> token --> cst
  token --> toc
  cst --> mask & lint & exports
  cst & lint & toc --> plate --> dish --> sefer
  mask & toc --> onion-book --> sous-core --> findings-snapshot --> sefer
```
