`galley/src/pantry.md` declares one `Budget` over three tiers (pinned, hot,
rebuildable) but enforces the ceiling on chunk products only. The other tiers
are counted against it and reported by `resident_bytes()`, never evicted. Each
store writes its own way; the point of this item is one write path that every
cache goes through, so the ceiling bites everywhere it can.

What to settle:

- Which stores are evictable at all. Pinned is pinned for a reason: placing a
  target's finding rescans its current text. Hot site rows and the
  Expediter's `derived::Store`s are the candidates.
- One insert/evict path that every store goes through, with eviction by the
  same last-touched LRU the chunk cache uses, or a per-tier order.
- A measurement first: resident bytes per tier over a whole Bible, with and
  without a reference, so the ceiling's default is a number and not a guess
  (`galley/tests/aggregate_accounting.rs` already holds the count to the real
  heap).

Not here, because they belong to the host: suppressions (the UI, keyed by
`sous-identity.ts`), edit/undo, document versions, reusing JS objects across
snapshots, and smoke tests in Sefer. Galley's own lifecycle is covered by the
churns in `galley/tests/equivalence.rs`: target edits, a moving reference, and
a drifting config.
