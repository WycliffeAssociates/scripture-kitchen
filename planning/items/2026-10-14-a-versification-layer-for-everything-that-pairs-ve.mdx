Will, on the pairing plan's versification question:

> Probably should have this layer of versification in the call stack for this. Probably should
> in sous/gallye too for anything that gets paired tbh.

Today a target and its source pair by their own book, chapter and verse numbers.
`sous_core::alignment::align` matches keys as written, and unpaired rows become alignment
facts, never findings. That is right when both sides share a versification, and it fails
quietly when they don't: a Psalm title counted as verse 1 on one side shifts the whole psalm,
and every unit after it pairs with its neighbour.

## Where it sits

<Calls id="where" title="one mapping step before any pairing">
```text
galley: a target book and a source book are both registered
  versification::to_org(book, chapter, verse) -> org key    -- the new layer, once per side @ mise (or galley)
  align(target rows, source rows)                           -- pairs org keys, unchanged @ sous-chef/core/src/alignment.rs:101
    sous: proportionality, source copy, …                   -- every paired sous rule
    pairing: Tables::build / update                         -- the alignment engine
  coordinates back to each side's own keys for display      -- a finding never shows an org number
```
</Calls>

- Each side declares its versification once (a `.vrs` name or a custom mapping) at
  registration. No sniffing.
- `org` is the pivot: BHS for the OT, UBS/NA for the NT. MACULA is keyed in `org` already, and
  WLC matches it in all 929 chapters. SBLGNT differs in one (ROM 16 ends at 24).
- Mapping happens once per side, at ingest. Every pairing consumer (sous and pairing alike)
  reads org keys, and only the display maps back.
