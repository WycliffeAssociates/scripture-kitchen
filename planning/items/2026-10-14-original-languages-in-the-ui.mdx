Pairing v1 uses Greek and Hebrew as a **hidden key**: lemma and sense ids that make the checks
work, with every label in the gateway language. This item is for when the originals become
something a user sees.

Things it would need, none started:

- **Surface words.** The lemma packs hold lemma, sense and Strong's, but not the inflected word
  as written. A Greek or Hebrew pane needs surface forms (a bigger pack) or the host's own
  original-language text, indexed word for word against MACULA's word ids.
- **Highlighting in a third pane.** The alignment buffer already carries the lemma and sense
  per source word, so linking a target word to its Greek word is a read, not new engine work.
- **Glosses and definitions** from the UBS dictionaries (SDGNT, SDBH; English, French, Spanish
  and Chinese), CC BY-SA 4.0.
- **Syntax trees.** MACULA ships them. They fit the same packs scheme, keyed by the same word
  ids.
- The plan's old rule still stands: messages observe and don't explain Greek. A lexicon in the
  UI is a product decision, not a check.
