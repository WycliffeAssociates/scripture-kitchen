`galley/sous-identity.ts` names a Sous finding so a saved suppression
survives edits and settings changes. Onion's lint diagnostics have nothing
like it, so Sefer cannot save "this is fine" for one.

Change, in the same module and contract:

- a site identity, `v1:o:<DiagnosticName>:<verse>:<flagged text>:<ordinal>`,
  escaped and ordinaled as the Sous ones are;
- a subject identity where a diagnostic has a subject: an unknown marker by
  its name ("always allow `\zqq`"). Which diagnostics have one is a small
  table, decided per name.

Pinned on the golden fixtures like the Sous identities; documented beside the
Sous ones in `galley/sous-messages.md`, "Stable identity".

Sefer side: `.sefer/onion-suppressions.json` keyed by it.
