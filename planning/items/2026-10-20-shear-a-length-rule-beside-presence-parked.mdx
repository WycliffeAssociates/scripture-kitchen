Presence landed (wire code 4); shear did not. Adjacent opposite length
extremes may mean content was divided differently, or may be real
translation difference. The proposal is in
[rules/presence-shear.md](sous-chef/rules/presence-shear.md).

Before it is a claim it needs declared adjacency, bridge compatibility,
opportunity counts, and counterexamples from genuine short/long neighbours.
The first action it would offer is "compare these neighbouring units
together", not relabelling verses.

This is the one unmet bullet of the old Stage 5 gate ("shear exclusions" in
the paired survey), which has nothing to exclude until the rule exists.
