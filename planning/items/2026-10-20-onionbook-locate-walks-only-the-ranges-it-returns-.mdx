`OnionBook::locate` (`galley/src/sous/onion_book.rs:74`) starts its
`SourceSpans` at mask range 0, and `next()` scans to the end of `mask.ranges`
after the last overlap. Draining one locate is O(ranges); locating every
finding is O(findings × ranges). On the hub's `hbo_uhb` proofread that was 90%
of 4 s (163,284 findings).

Change:

- Start at `mask.starts.partition_point(|&s| s <= from) - 1`, the way
  `galley/src/find.rs:275` already does, and return `None` once a projected
  start reaches `range.to()`.
- `pub fn mask(&self) -> &Mask`, so the hub drops its copy.

Check: a book of thousands of short verses; locate a range near the end and
drain it. Same runs as today, and `next()` touches only the ranges it returns
plus one.

Sefer side: none. The hub drops its mask copy.
