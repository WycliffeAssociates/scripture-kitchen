The vref loader (`BOOK C:V<TAB>text`) is a source producer today: `sous-cli
--source` takes it, with `<range>` placeholders fused into one interval unit.

Not built: the same loader as an addressable **target** projection, for fleet
work over vref corpora, whose offsets locate back to the vref line and typed
designator rather than pretending to be raw-USFM offsets. The fleet harnesses
(`examples/pattern_volume.rs`, `word_volume.rs`) build their own corpora
instead.
